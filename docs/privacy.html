ClassOps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-11

ClassOps is a classroom management tool operated by the instructor, Panupong, for use in courses he teaches. This policy explains what information ClassOps collects and how it is used.

Current pilot scope: the live v2 pilot currently supports Google sign-in and a minimal account screen only. Microsoft sign-in is not configured, and the browser does not yet offer classroom feature screens. Server-side classroom APIs exist for later development, but are not current browser features.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in information: when you sign in with Google, ClassOps receives your name and email address from that provider. ClassOps does not receive or store your password.
  • Course activity: if and when classroom functions are made available and used, this may include attendance records, exit-ticket responses, assignment submissions and grades, class feed posts and comments, and section join-code usage tied to your account.

How this information is used

Information collected is used solely to operate the class(es) you are enrolled in or teaching through ClassOps — taking attendance, recording grades, and supporting class communication. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside running the course.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on infrastructure operated directly by the instructor. It is not shared with third parties except Google, the sign-in provider used by the current pilot, and the network infrastructure (Cloudflare) used to serve the site securely over HTTPS.

Data retention

Course data is retained for the duration of the course and for a reasonable period afterward for grade-record purposes, then deleted. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting the instructor at the address below.

Security

ClassOps uses HTTPS for all traffic and does not store passwords (sign-in is handled entirely by Google). As a small, single-operator project, it does not carry a formal security certification; if you have a security concern, please report it to the contact below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to panupongsk@gmail.com.

ClassOps is an independent teaching tool and is not affiliated with Google or Microsoft.
